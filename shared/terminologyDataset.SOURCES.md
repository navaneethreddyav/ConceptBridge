# Terminology dataset sourcing

`terminologyDataset.json` is a curated, hand-built list — not a scrape/dump of any single document. This file records exactly what was verified against a fetched Osmania University (OU) source versus filled in from standard, well-established AICTE-aligned engineering curriculum knowledge, per the "prefer the latest official OU source, document the decision" instruction this dataset was built under.

## What was actually fetched and verified

- **`https://www.osmania.ac.in/Syllabua2024-2025/Engg/BE-Group%20A%20-I%20Year%20Syllabus.pdf`** and the equivalent **Group B** PDF (same directory) — official OU Faculty of Engineering first-year syllabus, w.e.f. AY 2024-25, common to all B.E. branches. Confirmed subject list used to ground first-year/foundational terms across all five domains: Engineering Mechanics, Engineering Physics, Engineering Chemistry, Basic Electrical Engineering, Programming for Problem Solving, Engineering Graphics, Engineering Workshop Practice, Environmental Sciences.
- **`https://methodist.edu.in/web/uploads/files/01%20BE%20(CSE)2020-24%20AY23-24%204th%20syllabus%2017AUG23.pdf`** — an OU-affiliated college's mirror of the official OU B.E. (CSE) VII/VIII semester syllabus (AICTE Model Curriculum, w.e.f. AY 2023-24; affiliated colleges are required to follow OU's syllabus verbatim, which is why this is treated as authoritative rather than a third-party summary). Confirmed full unit-wise topic breakdowns for: Distributed Systems, Computational Complexity, Advanced Operating Systems, Multimedia Technologies, Information Retrieval Systems, Big Data Analytics, Cloud Computing, Deep Learning, Information Security, Web Application Development. This is the direct source for most `cs` domain terms tagged with those subjects (e.g. Backpropagation, Convolutional Neural Network, MapReduce, ACID-adjacent transaction terms informed by the DBMS-adjacent unit content).
- **`https://www.uceou.edu/dept-mechanical.php`** — OU's University College of Engineering, Mechanical Engineering department page. Confirmed department focus areas and lab list used to ground the `mechanical` domain's advanced-subject terms: Design of Mechanical Systems, Manufacturing Technology, CAD/CAM, Finite Element Method, Robotic Engineering, Computational Fluid Dynamics, Non-Conventional Energy Sources, Composite Materials.
- **`https://www.osmania.ac.in/Syllabua2024-2025/Engg/`** (directory listing) — confirmed which branch-specific syllabus documents actually exist on OU's site as of this dataset's construction (e.g. `EEE VII and VIII sem.docx`, `4._ME_EEE_Power_Electronics_Final__09-Sep-2019_.pdf`), used only to confirm subject-area existence, not deep-parsed for unit content.

## What was not retrievable, and what was used instead

- OU's site does not expose a full II–IV year branch-specific B.E. syllabus for EEE, ECE, or Civil in an easily crawlable location (only the common first-year documents and a handful of individual electives were directly reachable). A second-year CSE document (`glwec.in`, an affiliated college) returned an HTML error page instead of a PDF and was not usable.
- For **EEE, ECE, Civil**, and the **CS** subjects outside the VII/VIII-semester document above (Data Structures, Operating Systems, DBMS, Computer Networks, Compiler Design, Theory of Computation, Digital Logic Design, Computer Organization), terms were filled in from standard, well-established AICTE-model-curriculum engineering content — the same curriculum framework OU's own fetched documents explicitly cite ("AICTE Model Curriculum"). These subjects and their core terminology are highly standardized across Indian engineering universities and were not fabricated or guessed; they reflect widely-taught, textbook-standard course content for each named subject.
- The Mechanical Engineering department page did not enumerate exact subject names for the foundational/core years (Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Strength of Materials, Theory of Machines) — those were filled in the same way, as standard subjects any AICTE-aligned Mechanical Engineering curriculum covers.

## Why this approach, not a full syllabus scrape

Two of OU's own PDFs (both first-year syllabus documents, ~32-40MB each) downloaded corrupted/truncated on the first attempt due to their size, and required a retry plus `qpdf` repair before they were parseable — OU's site is not reliably scrapable at scale in one sitting, and a from-scratch crawl of every branch's every-semester syllabus was not a practical use of this session. The terms in this dataset are accurate, standard engineering terminology regardless of source; the OU documents were used specifically to ground the **subject list** (what to cover, and roughly how much weight advanced/elective topics should get relative to foundational ones), not as the sole source of the terms themselves.

## Maintenance note

If OU publishes accessible II-IV year syllabi for EEE/ECE/Civil in the future, this dataset should be revisited to verify subject coverage against the actual official documents rather than the AICTE-standard fill-in used here.
